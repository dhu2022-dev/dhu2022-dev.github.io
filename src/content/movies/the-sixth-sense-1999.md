Great movie. It is a thriller with horror elements like death, the undead, deep psychology, and supernatural. But the characters acted mostly rationally, and not to spoil the movie but the child actor conveyed a deep depth of emotion and there were some deep heartwarming scenes showing a lot of empathy. Strangely relatable.
